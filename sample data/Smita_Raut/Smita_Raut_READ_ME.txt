STEP 3.  STUDENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Press "Get questions". The app writes 4 or 5 questions for this class and topic. They are not always the same,
   so read each question and answer in the same style as below.
   This school teaches in Marathi, so the app may write the questions in Marathi. You can answer in Marathi or in English.
   For each question press "Record Student A",
   say the answer out loud in a child's voice (10 to 20 seconds), and stop. Use Student B, C, D for the next children.

   Answers you can say (record them yourself):
   1. [Remember] If the app asks something like: "What did you learn today?"
      say: We learned how to separate things. We used a cloth to filter muddy water, and we heated salt water to get the salt.
   2. [Understand] If the app asks something like: "Explain what filtering does."
      say: Filtering keeps the big bits, like mud, on the cloth. Only clean water goes through.
   3. [Use it] If the app asks something like: "Where do you see filtering at home?"
      say: My mother filters tea with a strainer. The tea leaves stay in the strainer.
   4. [Think] If the app asks something like: "Why does the salt stay behind when water evaporates?"
      say: Because the water turns into vapour and goes up, but the salt cannot become vapour, so it stays in the pot.

   Press "Next: results".
