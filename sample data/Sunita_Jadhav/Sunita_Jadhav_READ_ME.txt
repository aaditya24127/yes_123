STEP 3.  STUDENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Press "Get questions". The app writes 4 or 5 questions for this class and topic. They are not always the same,
   so read each question and answer in the same style as below.
   This school teaches in Marathi, so the app may write the questions in Marathi. You can answer in Marathi or in English.
   For each question press "Record Student A",
   say the answer out loud in a child's voice (10 to 20 seconds), and stop. Use Student B, C, D for the next children.

   Answers you can say (record them yourself):
   1. [Remember] If the app asks something like: "What did you learn today?"
      say: We learned to subtract when the ones are not enough. We take one ten and break it into ten ones.
   2. [Understand] If the app asks something like: "Explain how to do fifty two minus twenty seven."
      say: Two is smaller than seven, so I break one ten into ten ones. Now twelve minus seven is five, and four tens minus two tens is two tens. The answer is twenty five.
   3. [Use it] If the app asks something like: "You have 41 marbles and give away 18. How many are left?"
      say: Twenty three. One is less than eight, so I break a ten. Eleven minus eight is three, three tens minus one ten is two tens.
   4. [Think] If the app asks something like: "A boy says 52 minus 27 is 35. Is he right? How do you know?"
      say: No. He took the smaller number from the bigger one in each place. Twenty five plus twenty seven is fifty two, so twenty five is right.
