STEP 3.  STUDENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Press "Get questions". The app writes 4 or 5 questions for this class and topic. They are not always the same,
   so read each question and answer in the same style as below. For each question press "Record Student A",
   say the answer out loud in a child's voice (10 to 20 seconds), and stop. Use Student B, C, D for the next children.

   Answers you can say (record them yourself):
   1. [Remember] If the app asks something like: "What did you learn today?"
      say: Percentage means out of one hundred. Fifty percent means fifty out of one hundred.
   2. [Understand] If the app asks something like: "Explain how to find a percentage in your own words."
      say: We write the part over the whole, multiply by one hundred. Like twelve out of twenty is twelve by twenty times hundred, so sixty percent.
   3. [Use it] If the app asks something like: "A shirt costs 500 rupees with 10 percent off. What do you pay?"
      say: Ten percent of five hundred is fifty. So I pay five hundred minus fifty, that is four hundred and fifty rupees.
   4. [Think] If the app asks something like: "Is 50 percent of 80 the same as 80 percent of 50?"
      say: I think yes, both are forty. But I am not sure why. Maybe because of multiplying.

   Press "Next: results".
