STEP 3.  STUDENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Press "Get questions". The app writes 4 or 5 questions for this class and topic. They are not always the same,
   so read each question and answer in the same style as below.
   For each question press "Record Student A",
   say the answer out loud in a child's voice (10 to 20 seconds), and stop. Use Student B, C, D for the next children.

   Answers you can say (record them yourself):
   1. [Remember] If the app asks something like: "What did you learn today?"
      say: Today we learned about ones, tens and hundreds. Ten sticks make one bundle, which is one ten.
   2. [Understand] If the app asks something like: "Explain place value in your own words."
      say: Place value means the place of the digit tells how much it is worth. In 34, the three is in the tens place, so it is thirty.
   3. [Use it] If the app asks something like: "Where can you use tens and ones at home?"
      say: When my mother counts rupees. A ten rupee note is one ten and two coins of one rupee are two ones.
   4. [Think] If the app asks something like: "Why is the 5 in 506 not fifty?"
      say: Because there is a zero in the tens place. So the five is in the hundreds place and it means five hundred.

   Press "Next: results".
