   A lesson that is all chanting. Expect: calm class and loud, confident chanting, but children cannot show what multiplication means (use sticks or stones first). The app also flags that a boy was threatened with standing outside and marks it as the first thing to talk about.

STEP 3.  STUDENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Press "Get questions". The app writes 4 or 5 questions for this class and topic. They are not always the same,
   so read each question and answer in the same style as below.
   This school teaches in Marathi, so the app may write the questions in Marathi. You can answer in Marathi or in English.
   For each question press "Record Student A",
   say the answer out loud in a child's voice (10 to 20 seconds), and stop. Use Student B, C, D for the next children.

   Answers you can say (record them yourself):
   1. [Remember] If the app asks something like: "What did you learn today?"
      say: We learned the three table and the four table. Three fours are twelve.
   2. [Understand] If the app asks something like: "What does three times four mean?"
      say: It means three fours, or four plus four plus four. That makes twelve.
   3. [Use it] If the app asks something like: "Four children each have three pencils. How many pencils?"
      say: Twelve pencils. I added three four times, because four children and each has three.
   4. [Think] If the app asks something like: "Why is three times four the same as four times three?"
      say: I do not know, madam. It is in the table.

   Press "Next: results".
