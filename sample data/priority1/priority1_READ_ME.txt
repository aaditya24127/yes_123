PRIORITY1: ADD YOUR OWN PRIORITY (a new reason to visit a school sooner)
======================================================================

THE IDEA
   "Schools where many students walk a long way to school should be visited sooner."
   Each school gets one number: the percentage of its students who live more than 3 km away.
   The higher the number, the sooner the school comes up in the list.

WHERE TO DO IT
   1. Open the first page (Priorities).
   2. Press the round "P" button at the top right (next to the search box). The priorities panel opens.
   3. Find the button "Create your own factor" (or "Add a factor") and press it. A window opens.

WHAT TO TYPE
   In the box at the top type exactly this and press "Suggest":

      Schools where many students walk more than 3 km to reach school should be visited sooner

   The app suggests a set-up. Check each box and change it to match this (the suggestion may differ a little):

      Factor name                       : Long walk to school
      The number each school needs      : Students living more than 3 km from school (%)
      How it raises priority            : More of it = visit sooner
      Unit (optional)                   : %
      At or below this = no extra priority : 10
      At or above this = full priority     : 50
      Applies to                        : Schools

THE NUMBERS TO ADD  (two ways, choose one)
   Way 1: type them. In the same window you see a box for every school. Type these numbers:

      School                                       Number
      ZP School, Wadgaon                           42
      Mahavir Jaideep English Medium School        12
      ZP School, Nandur                            55
      Rock Public School, Hinjewadi                18
      PCMC Primary School, Wakad                   8
      Shri Ganesh Vidyalaya, Ambegaon              36
      St. Mary's English School, Takali            22
      ZP School, Mhasoba Wadi                      61
      Sant Dnyaneshwar English School, Karanje     30
      New English School, Palshi                   15

   Way 2: upload a file. Press "Or upload Excel / CSV (school name + number)" and choose:
      priority1_school_values.csv   (it is in this folder and has the same numbers)

FINISH
   Press "Create and switch on". The new factor is switched on straight away (slider at 10).
   In the priorities panel (P) move its slider higher, for example to 30, to make it matter more, then press "Save priorities".

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
   * Scores go up for the schools with the highest numbers: "ZP School, Mhasoba Wadi" (61), "ZP School, Nandur" (55) and
     "ZP School, Wadgaon" (42). "Shri Ganesh Vidyalaya, Ambegaon" (36) moves up the list too.
   * Schools with small numbers (for example PCMC Primary School, Wakad: 8) get no extra priority, so they slide down a little.
   * Open a school's Details: the new factor appears with its number and says why.
   * The higher you put the slider, the bigger the change in the order.

TO UNDO IT
   Open the panel (P), find "Long walk to school", move its slider to 0 and press "Save priorities".
