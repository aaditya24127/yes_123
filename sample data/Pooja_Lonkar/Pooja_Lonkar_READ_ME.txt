STEP 3.  STUDENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Press "Get questions". The app writes 4 or 5 questions for this class and topic. They are not always the same,
   so read each question and answer in the same style as below.
   This school teaches in Marathi, so the app may write the questions in Marathi. You can answer in Marathi or in English.
   For each question press "Record Student A",
   say the answer out loud in a child's voice (10 to 20 seconds), and stop. Use Student B, C, D for the next children.

   Answers you can say (record them yourself):
   1. [Remember] If the app asks something like: "What did you learn today?"
      say: Our food comes from plants and from animals. Rice and jowar come from plants. Milk and eggs come from animals.
   2. [Understand] If the app asks something like: "Explain in your own words where milk comes from."
      say: Milk comes from cows and buffaloes. The farmer milks them and the dairy sells it in packets to the shop.
   3. [Use it] If the app asks something like: "Name one food at home and say if it is from a plant or an animal."
      say: Chapati is from wheat. Wheat is a plant, so chapati is from a plant.
   4. [Think] If the app asks something like: "Why do we also need the farmer for our food?"
      say: Because the farmer grows the plants and looks after the animals. Without the farmer there is no food in the market.

   Press "Next: results".
