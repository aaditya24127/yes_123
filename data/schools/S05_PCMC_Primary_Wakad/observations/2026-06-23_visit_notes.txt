BLOCK EDUCATION OFFICE, MULSHI, PUNE
Visit Report - PCMC Primary School, Wakad
=========================================

School: PCMC Primary School, Wakad
Board / Medium: Maharashtra State Board (SSC) / Marathi
Date of visit: 23 June 2026
Visited by: Mrs. Anjali Kulkarni, Cluster Resource Person, Hinjewadi-Mulshi Cluster 7
Lessons observed: Class 3 Marathi, Class 6 Science, Class 7 Mathematics
Teachers met: Mrs. Farhana Inamdar, Mrs. Smita Raut, Ms. Sunita Mane


SUMMARY OF THE VISIT
--------------------
I visited PCMC Primary School, Wakad on 23 June 2026 as part of my regular cluster visits and
observed 3 lessons. The Headmaster and the teachers concerned were present throughout the visit.


OBSERVATIONS
------------
- In Mrs. Farhana Inamdar's Class 3 Marathi lesson the teacher spoke for most of the period and
  students had very few chances to ask or answer questions. This is a significant concern that needs
  sustained support.
- No practical or demonstration was done in Mrs. Smita Raut's Class 6 Science lesson, and the
  science kit was lying unused. This is a significant concern that needs sustained support.
- Teaching-learning material available in the school was not used in Mrs. Farhana Inamdar's Class 3
  Marathi lesson; teaching was mostly from the textbook and blackboard. This is a minor area for
  improvement.

AGREED ACTIONS AND FOLLOW-UP
----------------------------
- Agreed action: Introduce question-and-answer rounds and pair work in every period. Responsible:
  Mrs. Farhana Inamdar. To be completed by 15 July 2026.
- Agreed action: Conduct one demonstration from the science kit every week. Responsible: Mrs. Smita
  Raut. To be completed by 17 July 2026.

NEXT STEPS
----------
I thank the Headmaster and the staff for their cooperation. I will review the progress of the agreed
actions during my next visit to the school.
