Visit Report - PCMC Primary School, Wakad
=========================================

School: PCMC Primary School, Wakad
Board / Medium: Maharashtra State Board (SSC) / Marathi
Date of visit: 3 July 2026
Visited by: Mrs. Anjali Kulkarni, Cluster Resource Person, Hinjewadi-Mulshi Cluster 7
Lessons observed: Class 3 Marathi
Teachers met: Mrs. Farhana Inamdar


SUMMARY OF THE VISIT
--------------------
I visited PCMC Primary School, Wakad on 3 July 2026 as part of my regular cluster visits and
observed 1 lessons. The Headmaster and the teachers concerned were present throughout the visit.


OBSERVATIONS
------------
- Children in Class 3 Marathi remained passive listeners throughout Mrs. Farhana Inamdar's lesson;
  questions from students were not encouraged. This is a significant concern that needs sustained
  support.
- Teaching-learning material available in the school was not used in Mrs. Farhana Inamdar's Class 3
  Marathi lesson; teaching was mostly from the textbook and blackboard. It is a small gap that
  regular practice can fix.

NEXT STEPS
----------
I thank the Headmaster and the staff for their cooperation. I will review the progress of the agreed
actions during my next visit to the school.
