BLOCK EDUCATION OFFICE, MULSHI, PUNE
Visit Report - St. Mary's English School, Takali
================================================

School: St. Mary's English School, Takali
Board / Medium: ICSE / English
Date of visit: 1 September 2026
Visited by: Mrs. Anjali Kulkarni, Cluster Resource Person, Hinjewadi-Mulshi Cluster 7
Lessons observed: Class 8 English, Class 5 Mathematics, Class 9 Mathematics, Class 7 Science
Teachers met: Mrs. Mary Gonsalves, Mrs. Mary Lobo, Mrs. Shweta Kamble, Mrs. Flavia D'Souza


SUMMARY OF THE VISIT
--------------------
I visited St. Mary's English School, Takali on 1 September 2026 as part of my regular cluster visits
and observed 4 lessons. The Headmaster and the teachers concerned were present throughout the visit.


OBSERVATIONS
------------
- In Class 8 English, students taught by Mrs. Mary Gonsalves could copy text but struggled to
  express their own ideas in writing. This is a significant concern that needs sustained support.
- Progress records and unit-test answer sheets for Class 5 Mathematics were not maintained properly
  by Mrs. Mary Lobo; marks had not been entered in the register. This is a significant concern that
  needs sustained support.
- Mrs. Mary Lobo taught the Class 5 Mathematics lesson using only the blackboard, although charts
  and other material were available in the school. It is a small gap that regular practice can fix.

AGREED ACTIONS AND FOLLOW-UP
----------------------------
- Agreed action: Give a daily five-sentence writing task with teacher feedback. Responsible: Mrs.
  Mary Gonsalves. To be completed by 12 September 2026.
- Agreed action: Update the progress register and submit unit-test marks to the CRP. Responsible:
  Mrs. Mary Lobo. To be completed by 12 October 2026.
- Agreed action: Use at least one teaching-learning material from the school stock in every period.
  Responsible: Mrs. Mary Lobo. To be completed by 20 October 2026.

NEXT STEPS
----------
I thank the Headmaster and the staff for their cooperation. I will review the progress of the agreed
actions during my next visit to the school.
