## 1. The project in plain words

**CRP Mentor Copilot** helps a *Cluster Resource Person* (CRP, a mentor of about 10-15 government / low-fee private schools in India,
Classes 1-10) decide **whom to visit first and why**, run the visit, keep records per school, and stay in touch with teachers.
Main aim of the hackathon entry ("AI for Foundational Learning"): good, simple, trustworthy decision support, not a dashboard of numbers.

What it does (details: `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` section 1):
1. **Priority list** of schools and teachers (0-100, 25 factors, every score links to the file and row it came from; the CRP can change weights and add factors; Reset / Delete priorities).
2. **Upload**: drop many files (Excel, CSV, Word, PDF, scans, photos, audio, zip). Sorted to school / teacher / kind / date, duplicates found, the CRP checks, then Import. Shows a **new school** panel (add school + teachers + data) or **no school found**; **Show allocation** table.
3. **Visit (4 steps)**: before class (brief, history, tabs) -> in class (record lesson, notes by typing / voice / photo / quick notes / translate to English) -> students (questions, record each answer as Student A, B...) -> results (AI evaluation; the mentor can check or change each AI verdict on a student answer). Saved into the records. A **teacher report PDF** (8-9 pages) can be made and sent.
4. **Teachers**: requests/tasks, notices, messages (the teacher app is separate).
5. **Textbooks** search/link, **Ask the copilot**, **Settings** (colours, layout, language, temporary "AI credits by key" card, log out), **Tutorial** (guided tour, text + voice).
6. **Teacher assessment (AI)**: on the Visit page, before the visit, the CRP presses *Start assessment* (5-10 questions). The teacher gets an alert, opens the teacher app and answers aloud: the app reads each question, the microphone turns on, *Done* -> "Noted" -> *Next* ... *Generate Report*. A report (scores 60% topic knowledge / 40% teaching method, confidence *indicators*, suggestions, do / don't, misconceptions, tools, speaking data, 3-5 visit focus points, charts, PDF) goes to the CRP and the teacher. A glowing circle (`VoiceOrb`) shows ready / listening / thinking / speaking in the app's colours on both sides.
7. **Chapter dropdown**: the topic of a visit / assessment is chosen from the chapters of the school's board (CBSE, Maharashtra SSC, ICSE), medium, class and subject (`GET /api/curriculum`, data in `backend/app/knowledge/curriculum.json`), with "Other (type it)".
8. **Report link + voice message (Results step)**: the CRP picks a language and presses *Send link in chat* (and optionally email). The teacher gets a **link in the chat of the teacher app** (with an alert) that opens the public page `/r` of the CRP site (no sign-in; the link carries a signed code): first **choose your language** (11), then **Report** (the written report, translated by the AI into that language, kept after the first time; *Save as PDF* uses the browser print) or **Your CRP's short review** (a voice message of 1 to 1:15 min written by the AI as if the mentor speaks, in that language, male or female Indian voice by the CRP's title; free `edge-tts` voices). Report and voice are prepared in the background as soon as a language is chosen (and right after sending), so the next screen opens quickly. The CRP card also has *See what the teacher will see*, *Copy link*, a voice preview and the English PDF download. There is **no WhatsApp** (removed on request).
9. **Why students did not understand**: the lesson evaluation also names, per student answer, the likely reason, and the Results step and PDF show it with what the teacher can do (`students.reasons`).
10. **Earlier visitors**: the Visit page shows what other mentors saw before (strengths, growth areas, reasons, notes; "Visited by ..." per visit) in the History tab, with a pointer in the Summary tab; the brief the AI writes uses it, and internal record names are never shown (`tidy_brief`).
11. **Visit header**: the name of the teacher being evaluated is shown on every step ("Evaluating ...").
12. **Sign-in pages**: both the CRP site and the teacher app have a two-panel sign-in (green brand panel with what the app does, form card; on phones a header and the card). A language button is in the corner of every sign-in page. The CRP page has show password; its 'New account' tab appears only when `ALLOW_SIGNUP=1`, and there is no demo fill-in any more (private sign-ins, section 2).
13. **Whole app in the chosen language** (CRP site and teacher app): every screen text is translated into the 10 other languages (see section 3 and the `tools/i18n` workflow in section 6). Data (names, school names, chapters, the AI's notes) stays as written.
14. **Voice assistant (voice mode)**: a *Voice mode* button in the top corner of every page (or Alt+V) switches the whole website to hands-free control, made for a blind CRP. A glowing circle (`VoiceOrb`) appears at the bottom right and animates listening / thinking / speaking. The CRP speaks (any of the 11 languages); Claude understands and answers aloud; the website really does it: open pages, show the priority list (schools / teachers, bands, search), open a school or teacher and read why it ranks where it does and the evidence, fill the Visit page (school, teacher, class, subject, chapter), start the visit, move between steps, add notes, ask a teacher for an assessment, explain a teacher's assessment scores, list teachers / subjects / chapters, change colours / layout / language ("switch to Marathi": the voice, the hearing and the website change at once), open any dropdown ("open the teacher dropdown": the choices are shown and read, then pick one by name or number), fill boxes and press buttons, say who teaches in a school, reset or save the priorities, send a chat message, sign out. The CRP is addressed as sir / madam in the greeting, in the goodbye and now and then, not in every answer. It **reads the screen** (how many questions were made, what the third one is about, the results, a card, the notes), **knows the whole website** (the four visit steps, every page, the assessment, the report link, itself; guide in `backend/app/knowledge/voice_guide.txt`), asks for a yes **only before big things** (sending to a teacher, saving priorities or the visit, assessment, sign out, delete) and changes **its own voice** (male / female, English accent, speed, volume, silent mode, ask-before-every-action). **Anything that changes something is shown on screen and needs a spoken or tapped yes first** ("Ask before every action" also covers opening pages). Details in `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` ("Voice assistant").
15. Records belong to the **school**, not to the mentor: a new mentor sees the whole history; each visit stores who made it (`mentor_name`).

Owner / main user: the account that owns the GitHub repo (section 2). Final goal: an Android / iOS app (Capacitor) later (`docs/MOBILE_APP.md`).

## 8. Decisions already made (do not reopen without asking)

- Recordings and photos used for notes or student answers are **never stored**, only the text. (Lesson recordings and Upload-page files are stored as source files.)
